Detailed usage and historical development notes. See [README](README.md) for the current overview.

The current **1.4.0 Training Budget** focuses solely on training costs and surplus/shortfall. Savings targets, the stock-target shortcut and savings timelines have been removed. Previously saved target values are ignored. Market-priced consumables and 30-point refill calculations remain included.

Version **1.4.0** adds market-priced training costs. Xanax uses catalogue market value when its override is blank. Enter **Refills per day** to budget **30 points per refill**, valued at the lowest current points-market unit asking price (or your point-price override). Other point spending is separate. Add any catalogue item with **+ Add training item**, including individual energy drinks, FHCs, happy boosters and other consumables; set average daily quantity and optionally override its price. Fractional quantities support weekly routines. Missing prices suppress surplus/timeline estimates rather than treating items as free. Existing manual refill spending remains for compatibility: clear it when switching the same expense to refill quantities. Points now refresh for every account. These are spending estimates, not a cooldown/training optimizer. Income totals remain unchanged.

Version **1.3.0** adds **Training budget**, a separate view with **Back to Income** navigation. Enter Xanax unit cost and daily quantity, daily refill spending, weekly rehab, daily rent/upkeep and other training costs. The target stays a fixed amount until changed. **Save budget** preserves inputs on the device, and changing accounts clears them along with other manual settings. Incomplete/stale income is flagged; projected daily equivalents are not daily cash payouts. Manual costs avoid assuming your training routine or current purchase prices.

Version **1.2.0** adds **Income → Next stock**. Replace your existing userscript with `torn-passive-income.user.js`, then Refresh. It ranks the next benefit increment for each dividend stock by **highest extra profit/day** (default), or **best daily return per dollar added**. An optional purchase budget identifies the highest-ranked affordable choice; other rows show the cash shortfall. Share thresholds account for doubling increment costs, partial holdings, and funded increments still waiting to mature. Recommendations use current catalogue share prices and the income panel's item-sale deduction and HRG estimate. Points pricing is now fetched even when you do not own PTS.

The ranking estimates ongoing dividend income after maturity, not share-price profits or immediate payouts. It compares individual next purchases, not an optimized portfolio of multiple purchases. Return per dollar uses new cash required; existing shares still have value. Passive perks such as TCI and non-saleable rewards are excluded. Missing values appear under unvalued stocks; stale snapshots are labelled. Refresh manually before a purchase. No trades or claims are performed.

Version **1.1.0** supports desktop/mobile Tampermonkey and **TornPDA** in the same file. Update the existing userscript in place to keep Tampermonkey settings. In TornPDA, add the full `torn-passive-income.user.js` file to its userscripts, enable it, and reload your profile or home page. Tap **Income → Settings**, enter your Full Access key, and **Save & refresh**. No GMforPDA helper is needed; it does not automatically take TornPDA's account key. Settings and keys do not sync between Tampermonkey and PDA.

Mobile controls have larger touch targets, safe-area spacing, 16px inputs to avoid focus zoom, and a scrollable panel with a sticky close button. TornPDA uses its native `PDA_httpGet` bridge and does not require the WebView's unreliable document-focus flag. Keep the page visible while refreshing. Native requests time out safely and ignore late responses.

Install **torn-passive-income.user.js** as a separate Tampermonkey script: create a new script, replace its contents with this file, and save. Open Torn and click the small **Income** button at the top right. In **Settings**, enter your own **Full Access API key**, then **Save & refresh**. Income always belongs to the key owner. A replacement key clears the previous snapshot and manual settings.

The panel shows an estimated **daily earning rate**, with a separate row and calculation basis for each source:

- **Stocks:** current API benefit increments × reward value ÷ API payout interval. Cash uses its actual interval (usually 31 days); items use Torn's current catalogue market value. All ten saleable item dividends are mapped by stock acronym and live item name. PTS uses 100 points per increment at the lowest unit asking price. Non-cash perks, energy, nerve, happiness and ammunition are shown separately. HRG requires an expected resale value per random property in Settings. New or unrecognized rewards remain unavailable instead of being guessed.
- **City bank:** the current investment's interest profit ÷ its original duration in days. Principal is excluded, and bank bonuses are already reflected in the reported profit. Matured investments retain the last cycle's rate with a reinvestment notice. Job specials that shorten investments are not modeled as a separate income source.
- **Cayman:** monthly interest ÷ the number of days in the current UTC month. The default projects from the current balance. Enter the month's lowest eligible balance for a better estimate, and set the Excursion and Oil Rig bonus checkboxes when applicable. These bonuses multiply. Update the minimum and bonus settings when circumstances change; this script does not reconstruct historical balance minima.
- **Rentals:** active contract daily rates on properties owned by the key owner. Vacant listings, rented accommodation owned by someone else and proposed lease extensions are excluded. Property pages are fetched sequentially, with incomplete pagination explicitly reported (maximum 10,000 properties). This is gross rental income; add any expenses you bear as negative custom cash entries.
- **Salary:** the latest recognized job/company pay log within 48 hours, or a daily override. The script discovers the pay log type through the API and reads only that type. Missing/old/unrecognized logs require an override. Recheck after changing employers or salary: a recent historical payment can differ from the current salary.
- **Director income:** latest daily company revenue minus employee wages, advertising and your entered average other daily costs. Enter stock purchases and other operating costs, including zero if none. Profit stays excluded until these costs are entered and the API supplies wages/advertising. Company profit can be negative. Director profit is a business earning estimate, not a cash withdrawal; no employee salary is added again.
- **Other recurring sources:** add as many named cash or item cycles as needed for job-point rewards, company perks, faction payments, private investments, virus sales or other arrangements. Enter amount/quantity and interval in days; item rows also take a Torn item ID and use live market prices. Negative cash entries represent expenses. These sources require your inputs and are not automatically inferred from account logs.

The optional item/point sale deduction adjusts estimated sale proceeds. It does not apply to cash dividends or the user-entered HRG resale estimate. Non-cash stock discounts are not separately monetized, avoiding double counting benefits already reflected in bank/company earnings. Share appreciation, gambling winnings, crimes, one-off gifts and other active/random proceeds are not automatically treated as recurring passive income. Dividends assume timely manual collection; unused claims do not keep accumulating. Rent and bank rates assume renewal to sustain the displayed daily equivalent.

**Refresh is manual**, from the panel or saving Settings. Opening the panel alone does not make requests. Data older than 15 minutes is marked stale. Five requests per rolling minute are allowed across this script's tabs on the same Torn origin. Tampermonkey uses Web Locks; TornPDA and browsers without Web Locks use atomic IndexedDB request reservations and a renewable refresh lease. Suspended/expired refreshes cannot keep sending requests or save an old snapshot. If coordination storage is unavailable, requests stop. Other scripts/devices have separate budgets. Most initial refreshes need about one minute, and larger property portfolios take longer. Keep browser tabs focused and visible (PDA only needs visibility). Authentication/rate-limit failures stop the refresh; independent missing sections produce an explicitly incomplete subtotal. No real key or authenticated account was used during development.

In Tampermonkey, keys and data stay in isolated userscript storage. In TornPDA, they use this script's prefixed **localStorage on Torn's origin**, which can be read by other scripts on Torn and is removed by clearing site data. Clearing site data also resets PDA's request budget. Keys are sent only to `api.torn.com` in the Authorization header; no external libraries, analytics, trades, claims or purchases. **Forget key & data** clears the key, income snapshot and manual inputs. Request-budget timestamps remain.

Development checks use mocked data. Real TornPDA device/live-account verification remains pending.
